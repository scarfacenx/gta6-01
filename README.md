An original open-world crime game for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. Explore a
sun-soaked modern city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the police as your
wanted level rises, and run missions through an original story — all in the
browser, no build step, no network.

## How to play

- **Goal:** work your way through the story missions, earn cash, and stay ahead of the law.
- **Move:** WASD to walk/run, mouse to look around. **E** to enter/exit a car, **Space** to brake, **Shift** to sprint.
- **Wanted level:** committing crimes raises your wanted level; police respond and chase you. Lose them to cool down.
- **Save:** progress saves automatically between sessions.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
